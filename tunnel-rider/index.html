How to ride

Three buttons. Total control.

The bike always moves forward. You decide when to leave the tunnel, how high to fly and how to land.

  • JumpTap at the tunnel edge to launch. Hold to stay in the air longer.
  • TurboHold for a burst of speed when you need to clear a long gap.
  • FlipHold to rotate the bike in mid‑air. Land level to keep riding.
