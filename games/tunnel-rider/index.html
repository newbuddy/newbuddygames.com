How to ride

Three buttons. Total control.

The bike always moves forward. You decide when to leave the tunnel, how high to fly and how to land.

  • Jump

    Tap at the tunnel edge to launch. Hold to stay in the air longer.

  • Turbo

    Hold for a burst of speed when you need to clear a long gap.

  • Flip

    Hold to rotate the bike in mid‑air. Land level to keep riding.

Features

Simple to start. Hard to master.

  • Jump between tunnels

    Read the next opening, same level, higher or lower, and time your launch from the edge.

  • Control flight and rotation

    Hold to glide further and rotate in mid-air so you land level.

  • Speed that keeps climbing

    The longer you survive, the faster you ride and the less time you have to react.

  • Five stages, 15 stars

    A new stage every 2,000 metres, each with up to three stars to earn.

  • A garage to build in

    Spend the coins you collect on bike liveries, rider outfits and upgrades.

  • Race, rank and challenge

    Race computer riders, join the optional global rankings or send a friend a challenge link.

Stages

Five worlds to master

Every 2,000 metres the scenery changes and the tunnels get trickier. Clear all five and the stages come round again, harder each time.

  1. Alpine Valley

    Where every run begins: mountain tunnels and open gaps that teach you the timing of a clean launch, flight and landing.

  2. Grand Bridge

    A long bridge crossing. Hop up onto the railings to collect double coins.

  3. Neon Night

    Neon-lit steel tunnels on scaffolds, ridden under the moon.

  4. Sunset Pass

    Tight tubes and quick hops: narrower tunnels and shorter gaps that punish a late jump.

  5. Frozen Peaks

    An ice cave. Pass under the icicles, then jump the spikes, with quick double hops over small gaps.

Garage

Build your ride

Collect coins on every run and spend them on new looks and upgrades that change how you fly.

  • Coin Magnet

    Pulls nearby coins into your path.

  • Glide

    Stronger hover while you hold jump.

  • Suspension

    Survive steeper, sloppier landings.

  • Lucky Coins

    Every coin has a chance to count double.

Bike liveries: Glacier Blue, Rally Red, Venom Green, Neon Violet and Desert Gold.
Rider outfits: Classic, Stealth, Sunrise and Arctic.

Compete

FAQ

Questions & answers

What kind of game is Tunnel Rider?

Tunnel Rider is a 2D side-view motorcycle arcade game. Your bike always moves forward; you choose when to leave each tunnel, how long to stay in the air and how to rotate so you land level in the next opening.

Which platforms is Tunnel Rider available on?

Tunnel Rider is being made for Android and is coming soon to Google Play. We will add the store link to this page when it is live.

How do the controls work?

There are three buttons. Jump: tap at the tunnel edge to launch and hold to stay in the air longer. Turbo: hold for a burst of speed. Flip: hold to rotate the bike in mid-air, then land level to keep riding.

How many stages are there?

There are five stages: Alpine Valley, Grand Bridge, Neon Night, Sunset Pass and Frozen Peaks. Each is 2,000 metres long and awards up to three stars, for 15 stars in total. After Frozen Peaks the stages come round again, harder each time.

Do I need an account to play?

No. Your progress, coins and settings are saved on your device. The Global Rider Rankings are optional; if you join them, the game uses an anonymous account and only the rider name and country you choose are shown publicly.

What is a challenge link?

When you challenge a friend, the game creates a link with your rider name, country, stage, score and distance and opens your device’s share menu. Your friend can then ride the same stage and try to beat your score.

How do I delete my rankings profile?

Email faisalabb67@gmail.com with your rider name and we will delete your rankings profile and scores within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device.

How do I get help with the game?

Email faisalabb67@gmail.com. Please include your device model and, if it is about the rankings, your rider name.
