Tunnel Rider Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Tunnel Rider game for Android ("the game", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Tunnel Rider is made by NewBuddy.

The short version

  • We never ask for your real name, email address, phone number, location or contacts.
  • Your progress, coins and settings stay on your device.
  • If you use the online Global Rider Rankings, the rider name and country you choose, and your best scores, are stored on our server and shown publicly in the rankings.

Information stored only on your device

Your game progress (stages cleared, scores, coins, garage items and settings) is saved on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Online rankings

The Global Rider Rankings are optional. When you open the rankings or join them, the game creates an anonymous account for your install. This account has a random ID and no email, password or personal details.

If you join the rankings, we store:

InformationWhyWho can see it
Rider name you choose (3–16 letters or numbers)To show you in the rankingsPublic, to all players
Country you chooseFor the country rankingsPublic, to all players
Best distance, best score, stages cleared and number of runsTo rank riders and check scores are genuinePublic, to all players (number of runs is not shown)
Anonymous account ID and the time of your last runTo keep your results together and prevent cheatingNot shown to players

Please don't use your real name or other personal details as your rider name.

Challenge links

When you choose to challenge a friend, the game creates a link containing your rider name, country, stage, score and distance, and opens your device's share menu. The link is only shared with the people and apps you pick. We don't receive a copy of it.

Service providers

The online rankings are hosted by Supabase, which stores the information above for us. Like any internet service, Supabase may briefly keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the service secure and working. The game is distributed through Google Play, which has its own privacy policy.

We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Rankings information is kept while you are in the rankings. If you ask us to delete it, we remove your profile and scores.

Deleting your information

To have your rankings profile and scores deleted, email us at faisalabb67@gmail.com with your rider name. We will delete them within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device.

Children

The game does not ask for personal information. Because rider names are public, we ask players under 13 to get a parent's permission before joining the online rankings. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a rider name, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: faisalabb67@gmail.com
