Focus Circles

An upcoming casual tracking game from NewBuddy Games. Keep your eye on one ball as the circles shuffle and move, then pick out the one you started with.

Ask to hear when it launches How it works
The idea

Watch closely. Don’t blink.

Focus Circles is about tracking: a simple test of attention and reflexes that’s easy to understand and hard to get perfect.

  1. Spot the target

    One ball is marked at the start. Lock your eyes on it.

  2. Follow the shuffle

    The mark disappears and the identical balls shuffle and move around.

  3. Pick it out

    When they stop, tap the ball you think is your original target.
